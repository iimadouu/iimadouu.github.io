The approach

What this actually looks like.

Most of your customers are on Android, on a mid-range phone, on a connection that is not always fast. That shapes every decision: the app is built for small screens, offline states, compressed assets and battery life rather than for a demo on a flagship device.

I build with Flutter, which gives you one codebase running natively on both Android and iOS. That means a single budget, a single set of fixes and identical behaviour on both platforms — the approach behind the Archiver and stock-management software I have shipped.

Apps range from a simple informational app to a full product with accounts, notifications, sync and dashboards. I handle the whole path: interface, backend connection, testing on real devices, and the store release.

Questions

Asked, answered.

Do you build for both Android and iOS?

Yes. Flutter produces one app that runs on both, so a single project covers Android and iPhone instead of two separate budgets.

How much does a mobile app cost in Algeria?

A basic static app is 8,000–12,000 DZD. Apps connected to Firebase with storage sit at 15,000–25,000 DZD. Full-featured apps with login, notifications and dashboards are 30,000–60,000 DZD, and on-demand apps with a custom backend start at 80,000 DZD.

Will you publish the app to the Play Store?

I prepare the release build and store assets and walk you through submission. The developer account stays in your name so you keep ownership, and I guide you through the review requirements.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes, when it makes sense. Offline handling is designed in from the start for apps used in the field, in shops or on unstable connections.

Can the app use my existing website or backend?

Yes. If you already have an API or Firebase project, the app connects to it. If not, I build the backend needed for accounts, data and notifications.
